Need help installing?

Android

  1. Tap Download Uniride app, then open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then go back and tap Install.
  3. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. Works on Android 7.0 or newer.

iPhone

  1. Open Uniride for iPhone in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Uniride from your home screen, just like any other app.
